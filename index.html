Why we’re here

Most of us have been running our whole lives, chasing, fixing and pushing through, until the body says: stop.

Stop Feel Breathe grew out of my own recovery from burnout, guided by the teachings of mindfulness and Zen. What healed me wasn’t doing more. It was learning to stop, to feel instead of fight, and to rest in the simple breath. These practices are here for you too.

Three simple steps

1

Stop

Put down what you’re holding. Let the to-do list wait. There’s nothing to achieve here and nowhere to get to. You have already arrived. Healing begins when you stop running.

2

Feel

Come out of your head and into your body. Notice what’s here: tension, buzzing, tiredness, sadness, joy. Don’t fight it or fix it. Let it be. Hold it gently, the way you’d hold a crying child.

3

Breathe

Rest in your natural breath. There’s no need to control it. Let it be your anchor when the mind runs off, and simply enjoy it. Breathing in, you embrace your pain with tenderness. Breathing out, you let it soften.

Breathe with us

Follow the circle: breathe in as it grows, out as it softens. If it feels forced, let the circle go and let your breath find its own rhythm. One breath is enough.
