Why we’re here

Most of us have been running our whole lives, chasing, fixing and pushing through, until the body says: stop.

Stop Feel Breathe™ grew out of my own recovery, guided by the teachings of mindfulness and Zen. What healed me wasn’t doing more. It was learning to stop, to feel instead of fight, and to rest in the simple breath. This method is here for you too.

The method

1

Stop

Put down what you’re holding. Let the to-do list wait. There’s nothing to achieve here and nowhere to get to. You have already arrived. Healing begins when you stop running.

2

Feel

Come out of your head and into your body. Notice what’s here: tension, buzzing, tiredness, sadness, joy. Don’t fight it or fix it. What you resist, persists. Let it be. Embrace it gently, hold it the way you’d nurture a crying child.

3

Breathe

While embracing what’s inside, rest in your natural breath. There’s no need to control the breath. Let it be your anchor when the mind runs off, and simply enjoy it. See if you can gently breathe into the tension. Breathing in, you embrace your pain with tenderness. Breathing out, you let it soften. Over time, this practice will heal you.

Breathe with us

Follow the circle: breathe in as it grows, out as it softens. If it feels forced, let the circle go and let your breath find its own rhythm. One breath is enough.
